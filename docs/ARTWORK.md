# Artwork provenance

The anonymous speaker-creature illustrations in the app's asset catalogue were
generated with an image model during development. They depict original hybrid
creatures such as a mushroom fox, cloud whale and teapot snail. These are illustrated
assets, not a validated speaker-identity system or an authentication mechanism.

As far as rights are held in the generated artwork, it is distributed under
Apache-2.0 with the source. Preserve the project's applicable notices.

The orb is rendered by the project's shaders. Listen is an independent project;
these assets do not grant rights to any third party's names, games or artwork.
